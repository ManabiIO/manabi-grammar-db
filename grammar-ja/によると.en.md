# によると

## Meaning

**によると** introduces the source of reported information: “according to.” The source can be a person, an organization, a report, a forecast or a study. **によれば** has a similar reporting use, and **によりますと** is a polite form often heard in news reports.

- 天気予報によると、明日は雨だそうです。 — According to the weather forecast, it will rain tomorrow.
- 調査によれば、利用者が増えている。 — According to the study, the number of users is increasing.

The source phrase ends immediately before the reporting expression; the information that follows is what that source reports. Keep the source and the reported statement separate when reading the highlights.

### Similar forms

**による** can also express cause or another relationship, and **と** can introduce a quotation or condition. Those uses do not necessarily mean “according to.” Matching this reporting construction provides evidence for the form; it does not verify the statement or resolve every possible interpretation without context.
