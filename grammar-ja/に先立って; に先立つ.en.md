# に先立って; に先立つ

## Meaning

### Matching scope

This rule recognizes the reviewed attachment and marker forms. Its context component is only the observed ending token, or the explicitly reviewed question ending; it does not recover an entire phrase or clause. The full て marker and the basic-form adjectival 先立つ are distinct paths. The incomplete stem 先立っ is not an independent occurrence.

Form evidence is tentative. Literal and grammatical readings can share these words, so the surrounding context determines the intended sense. Only the listed spellings and tokenizations are supported; other endings need separate review. Punctuation and blank paragraphs cannot join the attachment to the marker.
