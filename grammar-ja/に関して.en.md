# に関して

## Meaning
**名詞 + に関して** introduces the subject matter of a statement: “concerning …” or “with regard to …”. It is common in formal explanations and reports.

The attributive form **名詞 + に関する + 名詞** modifies a following noun, as in **税金に関する本**. The current detector requires the topic noun, and also the following noun for the attributive form. The selected range identifies the relational expression; its surrounding nouns supply attachment evidence.
