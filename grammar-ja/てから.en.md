# てから
## Meaning

With a verb in the **て-form**, **てから** places the next action after that event: **食べてから** means “after eating.”

The linked Takoboto reference also covers **noun + から**, which identifies a starting point or source, such as a time, place or person. These starting-point uses are included on this page alongside the sequential use of **てから**. Predicate **から** meaning “because” is a separate construction.
