# 次第
## Meaning

**Verb stem + 次第** means “as soon as”: the following action occurs once the first event is complete.

**Noun + 次第** means “depending on”: the outcome depends on the named condition. It can end in **だ**, **です** or **である**, connect to another clause with **で**, or stand without a copula in a short statement.

Both uses appear in the linked Takoboto reference. Gradual **次第に** (“gradually”), indiscriminate **手当たり次第に**, and the ordinary noun **次第** (“circumstances”) are distinct uses.
