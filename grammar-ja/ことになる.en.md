# ことになる

## Meaning

**Verb plain form + ことになる** can describe an arrangement, a decision viewed as an outcome, or what follows from a situation. Negative verbal context is also possible.

Context distinguishes an externally imposed result, an agreement, and an expected consequence. Compare **ことにする** for a decision presented as an action.

### Focus and standing arrangements
**は** or **も** can follow **に** to add contrast or inclusion. **ことになっている** can describe a standing arrangement or rule, rather than a new decision.
