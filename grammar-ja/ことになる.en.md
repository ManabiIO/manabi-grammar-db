# ことになる
## Meaning
**Verb plain form + ことになる** can describe an arrangement, a decision viewed as an outcome, or what follows from a situation. Negative verbal context is also possible.

A surface match does not establish whether the result was imposed externally, agreed jointly, or simply expected. Compare ことにする for a decision presented as an action.

### Parts and focus particles
The observed attached verb is a separate component. For a negative attachment,
that component includes the verb stem and **ない**. The main construction selects
**こと**, **に**, and the observed final verb form. It does not reconstruct the
entire preceding clause or append every later tense or aspect ending.

A single **は** or **も** between **に** and the final verb is supported as a
separate focus component. The main construction retains separate ranges on
either side of that particle. This does not turn intervening words into part of
the marker. Other particles and punctuation require separately reviewed forms.

The same core can appear with further inflection, such as **ことにしている**
or **ことになっている**. A core match alone does not settle an ongoing practice,
a standing arrangement, or the identity of a decision maker. Confidence remains
unassessed. Sentence and quotation boundaries cannot be crossed.
