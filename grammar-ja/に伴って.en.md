# に伴って

## Meaning

### Matching scope

This rule recognizes the reviewed noun-linked form and exposes the final observed noun token separately from the marker. That token may be only the ending of a compound noun; it is not a recovered whole phrase. The reviewed marker requires its case particle, verb lemma and specified ending. Other endings and kana spellings need separate review.

Form evidence is tentative. The construction can share wording with literal readings; the surrounding sentence determines the intended meaning. Punctuation and blank paragraphs cannot bridge the noun and marker. Compare the externally resolved explanations below for source-specific senses.
