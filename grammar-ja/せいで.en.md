# せいで

## Meaning
**せいで** presents a reason for an unwanted result, often assigning blame: “because of …” or “due to …”. Whether the speaker is blaming someone, joking, or describing a practical cause depends on context.

The construction can follow a plain clause, an adjective, or a noun linked with **の**. The current detector recognizes attached **せいで・所為で**, including supported past and negative endings. A separate occurrence of the noun **せい** does not establish this construction. The source explanations below provide additional examples and usage distinctions.
