# せいで

## Meaning

**せいで** presents a reason for an unwanted result, often assigning blame: “because of …” or “due to …”. Whether the speaker is blaming someone, joking, or describing a practical cause depends on context.

The construction can follow a plain clause, an adjective, or a noun linked with **の**. **所為で** is another spelling. Compare **おかげで**, which usually presents an outcome as beneficial.
