# ように言う

## Meaning

**Verb plain form + ように言う** can report telling someone to do something. A negative verb can report telling someone not to do it. **先生は本を読むように言った。** reports an instruction to read a book.

Passive **本を読むように言われた。** means being told to read a book. Tense and politeness can vary with the form of **言う**.

### Contrast
**そのように言った。** means saying something in that way. **読むようにした** describes an effort or practice, and **読むようになった** describes a change; the different final predicates distinguish these constructions.
