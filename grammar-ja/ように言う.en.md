# ように言う
## Meaning
**Verb plain form + ように言う** can report telling someone to do something. A negative verb can report telling someone not to do it. **先生は本を読むように言った。** reports an instruction to read a book.

The selected range covers **ように** and the observed form of **言う**. The attached verb is a separate component. Passive reporting, such as **読むように言われた**, retains the visible **言わ** portion; passive and tense endings can have their own grammar matches.

### Contrast and coverage
**そのように言った。** means saying something in that way; the demonstrative does not supply the required verbal instruction attachment. **読むようにした** and **読むようになった** use different final predicates and belong to other rules.

The detector supports plain and negative verbal attachments with **言う**, including its kana spelling. It does not determine the speaker’s intent, reconstruct a whole quoted clause, or cover every instruction verb. Confidence remains unassessed.
