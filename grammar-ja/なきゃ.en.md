# なきゃ

## Meaning
**なきゃ** and **なけりゃ** are conversational forms of **なければ**. They introduce a negative condition. **時間がなきゃ、明日にしよう** means “If there's no time, let's do it tomorrow.”

The same form can end the sentence when the requirement is understood. **明日は早く起きなきゃ** means “I have to get up early tomorrow.” You can also say the full **起きなきゃいけない**.
