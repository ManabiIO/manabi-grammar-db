# ている

## Meaning

A verb in the **て/で form + いる** can describe an action in progress, a continuing result, or a repeated activity. The intended reading depends on the verb and context.

The ending can be polite, past, or negative: **読んでいます**, **読んでいた**, and **読んでいない** are common examples.

In conversation, **ている / でいる** are often shortened to **てる / でる**. Related past contractions include **てた / でた**.
