# ている
## Meaning
A verb in the **て/で form + いる** can describe an action in progress, a continuing result, or a repeated activity. The intended reading depends on the verb and context.

This matcher covers common plain, polite, past and negative forms, plus plain **てる/でる** contractions where the active parser identifies the contracted auxiliary. It does not decide whether the situation is progressive or a resulting state.

Contractions are covered only when the parser retains an identifiable **てる/でる** auxiliary. For example, it analyzes **読んでた** without that auxiliary, so this rule currently leaves that form unmatched. Contracted negatives and compound auxiliary constructions are outside the authored coverage.
