# とみられる

## Meaning

A clause followed by **とみられる / と見られる** can present an assessment: something is considered or expected to be the case. News reporting often uses it to describe a conclusion drawn from the available information. The expression does not itself identify who made the assessment or establish that the reported proposition is true.

The assessment can be polite, past, or negative. A negative inside the reported clause, as in **再開しないとみられる**, differs from negating the assessment itself, as in **故障だとみられない**.

### Contrast
Literal **鳥を見られる** concerns seeing a bird, while **読んでみる** means trying to read. Their forms resemble parts of the reporting expression but convey different meanings.
