# まま

## Meaning
**まま** describes a state that continues without the change someone might expect: “still …”, “as it is”, or “without changing …”. Common attachments include a past or negative verb form, an adjective, and **名詞 + の**.

The current detector covers these finite attachments and demonstrative expressions such as **そのまま** and **このまま**. It does not treat an isolated mention of the word as a construction. Its attachment evidence does not identify the full surrounding clause or decide whether leaving the state unchanged was intentional.
