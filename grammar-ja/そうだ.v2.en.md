# そうだ
## Meaning
**Verb stem or adjective stem + そう** expresses an appearance or an apparent likelihood. It can continue with だ/です, modify a noun with な, or be adverbial with に. The exceptional **よさそう** form is included.

This is distinct from hearsay そう after a plain clause. The rule requires the suffix analysis supplied by the active parser; it does not infer appearances from arbitrary occurrences of the word そう.
