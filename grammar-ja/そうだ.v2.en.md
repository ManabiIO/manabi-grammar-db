# そうだ

## Meaning

**Verb stem or adjective stem + そう** expresses an appearance or an apparent likelihood. It can continue with **だ / です**, modify a noun with **な**, or be adverbial with **に**. **よい / いい** has the exceptional form **よさそう**.

This differs from hearsay **そうだ** after a plain clause: appearance describes an impression, while hearsay reports information from another source.
