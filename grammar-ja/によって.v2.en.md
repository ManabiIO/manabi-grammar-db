# によって

## Meaning

**によって** connects a noun phrase to an event or description. Depending on the sentence, it can indicate a cause (“because of”), a means (“by means of”), an agent in a passive sentence (“by”), or something on which a result varies (“depending on”). **により** is common in formal writing. **による** connects the same kind of phrase directly to a following noun.

- 地震によって建物が壊れた。 — Buildings were damaged by the earthquake.
- 地震による被害 — Damage caused by the earthquake.
- 人によって考え方が違う。 — Ways of thinking differ from person to person.

The attached phrase may itself contain particles and modifiers. In **企業への不正アクセスによる情報の流出**, the cause is **企業への不正アクセス** (“unauthorized access to a company”), and the following noun phrase is **情報の流出** (“the leak of information”).

The expression's form does not settle which meaning applies. Read the complete sentence and the source explanations below when distinguishing cause, means, agent and variation.
