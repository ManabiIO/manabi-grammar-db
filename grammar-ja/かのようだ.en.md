# かのようだ
## Meaning
**Clause + かのようだ** compares an appearance or behavior with a hypothetical situation: “as if …”. The situation need not be true. **すべて知っているかのようだ。** presents the appearance of knowing everything.

### Recognized parts
The detector checks an observed finite predicate or specified past/negative clause ending before **かのよう**. It returns that ending as context, **かのよう** as the comparison marker, and the following **だ**, **に**, or **な** as a separate ending component. The main selection includes both marker and ending; preceding clause context is separate.

**だ** may be inflected, as in **かのようだった**. **かのように** can modify a following action, while **かのような** can modify a noun. The detector does not reconstruct the entire preceding clause or resolve the figurative interpretation. Confidence remains unassessed.

### Contrast
**誰かのようだ。** means “like someone”. The **か** belongs to the indefinite expression **誰か**, and there is no finite clause attachment for this **かのようだ** rule. Likewise, **本のようだ** lacks **か**. These may involve other comparison grammar; they are not matches for this specific rule.
