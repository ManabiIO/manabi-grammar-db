# かのようだ

## Meaning

**Clause + かのようだ** compares an appearance or behavior with a hypothetical situation: “as if …”. The situation need not be true. **すべて知っているかのようだ。** presents the appearance of knowing everything.

**かのように** modifies an action, while **かのような** modifies a noun. The final **だ** can be inflected, as in **かのようだった**.

### Contrast
**誰かのようだ。** means “like someone”: its **か** belongs to the indefinite expression **誰か**. **本のようだ** means “like a book”. These use the comparison **のようだ**, rather than presenting a hypothetical clause with **かのようだ**.
