# ようにする

## Meaning

**Verb plain form + ようにする** describes making an effort, arranging matters, or adopting a practice so that something happens. A negative verb can describe trying to avoid an action.

**毎朝走るようにしている。** describes making a practice of running every morning. **ようにした** can describe a deliberate step or change in practice; it does not by itself establish whether the effort succeeded.

Compare **ようになる**, which describes a change in a situation, habit, or ability.
