# ようにする
## Meaning
**Verb plain form + ようにする** describes making an effort, arranging matters, or adopting a practice so that something happens. A negative verb can describe trying to avoid an action.

In **朝に走るようにした。**, **走る** is the attached verb and **ようにし** is the selected construction. The ending of **する** can vary; matching the form does not establish whether the effort succeeded. **ようになった** instead describes a change and belongs to **ようになる**.

### Coverage
The current rule checks a plain verb or a negative verb immediately before **ようにする**. It exposes the observed verbal attachment separately from the marker. Noun comparisons such as **上例のようにする。**, demonstratives, and quoted strings are outside this rule. Past attachments and other intervening material need separately reviewed forms; confidence remains unassessed.
