# か〜ないかのうちに

## Meaning
This expression describes a second event that happens just as the first event takes place, or before the first event has fully happened. The same verb appears on both sides of the first **か**, with **ない** on the second occurrence.

**ベルが鳴るか鳴らないかのうちに、子どもたちは教室を飛び出した。** The children rushed out of the classroom as soon as the bell rang.
