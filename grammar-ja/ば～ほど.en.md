# ば～ほど

## Meaning

Repeating a verb in its **ば** conditional form and plain form before **ほど** expresses a proportional relationship: “the more …, the more …”. A な-adjective can also use **なら** without explicit **ば**, followed by the same adjective with **なほど**. The sentence gives the full meaning.

**読めば読むほど面白くなる。** means “The more I read, the more interesting it becomes.” The repeated verb describes the action whose extent is linked to the following change.

**静かなら静かなほど落ち着く。** means “The quieter it is, the more relaxing it feels.”
