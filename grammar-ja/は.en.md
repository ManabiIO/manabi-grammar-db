# は
## Meaning
The particle **は** presents the topic of a sentence and contrasts with が by framing what follows as known or background information.

It can follow another particle, an adverb, or a quoted phrase. Matching checks the topic particle's morphology and JMdict identity; it does not require an immediately preceding noun or claim to identify the full topic phrase.
