# については

## Meaning
**名詞 + については** makes a topic of what the following statement concerns: “as for …” or “regarding …”. The added **は** can set the topic apart from other matters; the particular contrast depends on context.

The selection includes both **について** and **は**, with a preceding noun required as attachment evidence. The broader **について** construction may also be recognized in the same expression. These are overlapping explanations of the source text, rather than two separate pieces of text.
