# ことはない

## Meaning

**Verb dictionary form + ことはない** often says that there is no need to do something. The polite form is **ことはありません**.

Depending on context, similar wording can instead describe the absence of an occasion or fact. **行ったことはない** denies past experience: “I have never been.” The preceding verb form helps distinguish that from reassurance that an action is unnecessary.
