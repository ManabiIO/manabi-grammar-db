# たり する　form

## Meaning
**～たり～たりする** lists example actions or states: “do things such as … and …”. A single **たり** item can also give one example without claiming that it is the only activity. The voiced form **だり** appears with appropriate verb endings, as in **読んだり**.

Each detected item has its own predicate and listing-marker ranges, with an attachment relation between them. Supported items include finite verbs, adjectives, negative forms, nominal states, and explicit voice suffixes. The current rule does not infer a complete coordinated clause or its shared objects. Words such as **足りない** and **当たり前** are excluded.
