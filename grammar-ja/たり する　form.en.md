# たり する　form

## Meaning

**～たり～たりする** lists example actions or states: “do things such as … and …”. A single **たり** item can also give one example without claiming that it is the only activity. The voiced form **だり** appears with appropriate verb endings, as in **読んだり**.

Examples can be verbs, adjectives, negative expressions, or nominal states. The final **する** carries tense or politeness for the list, as in **本を読んだり音楽を聞いたりした。**: “I did things like reading books and listening to music.”
