# ことにする

## Meaning

**Verb plain form + ことにする** presents a decision to do something; a negative verb presents a decision not to do it. The final **する** can be inflected.

This is distinct from **ことになる**, which can frame an arrangement or outcome rather than the speaker's own decision.

### Focus and ongoing practice
**は** or **も** can follow **に** to add contrast or inclusion. **ことにしている** can describe an established personal practice, while **ことになっている** commonly presents a standing arrangement. Context identifies who made a decision and whether it has been carried out.
