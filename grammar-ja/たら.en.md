# たら

## Meaning

**たら** introduces a condition. It can mean “if” or “when.” **雨が降ったら休みます** means “If it rains, we'll take a break.” **家に着いたら連絡します** means “I'll get in touch when I get home.”

For a verb or い-adjective, use the plain past form and add **ら**, as in **歩いた → 歩いたら**, **読んだ → 読んだら**, or **安かった → 安かったら**. With a noun or な-adjective, use **だったら**, as in **定休日だったら**.

Longer predicates keep their endings before **ら**. **大雨でなかったら** means “if it isn't raining heavily,” and **お時間がありましたら** is a polite way to say “if you have time.”
