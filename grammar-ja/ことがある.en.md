# ことがある
## Meaning
**Verb plain form + ことがある** can describe something that happens occasionally. With a **た-form verb**, it commonly expresses past experience. A negative verb before こと can describe occasions when an action does not happen.

These readings share a construction; the surrounding tense and context matter. The rule requires verbal context and does not label a standalone noun phrase such as 大切なことがある as this grammar point.
