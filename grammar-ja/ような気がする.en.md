# ような気がする
## Meaning
**ような気がする** expresses a tentative impression, expectation, or uncertain recollection. **雨が降るような気がする。** presents rain as the speaker’s impression rather than a settled claim.

### Recognized parts
The selected construction is **ような気がする**; **する** may be inflected. Its observed attachment is a separate component. The current rule supports a plain verb or adjective ending, specified past/negative endings, a noun + **の**, an adjectival noun + **な**, and demonstratives such as **その**.

A context component can contain only the visible clause ending. In **読んだような気がする。**, it records **だ** as the observed past ending; it does not pretend to recover the whole preceding clause. With **本当のような気がする**, it records **本当の**.

### Contrast and limits
**読むような気持ちだ。** lacks the fixed **気がする** predicate and does not match this rule. Sentence and quotation boundaries cannot be crossed to manufacture an attachment. Other inflections and clause forms need separate review. Confidence remains unassessed; the detector does not evaluate whether the impression is correct.
