# ような気がする

## Meaning

**ような気がする** expresses a tentative impression, expectation, or uncertain recollection. **雨が降るような気がする。** presents rain as the speaker's impression rather than a settled claim.

The final **する** can be inflected. **読んだような気がする。** can express an uncertain recollection of having read something.

### Contrast
**ような気持ちだ** describes a feeling using **気持ち**, rather than the fixed expression **気がする**. Similar wording does not always carry the same nuance.
