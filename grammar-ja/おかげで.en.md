# おかげで

## Meaning
**おかげで** connects a reason with an outcome that the speaker presents as beneficial: “thanks to …”. Context can make the expression ironic, so its presence alone does not establish whether the outcome is good.

It can follow a plain clause, an adjective, or a noun linked with **の**. The current detector covers these attachments and the spellings **おかげ・お陰・お蔭**, followed by **で**. Its selections identify the cause marker; the preceding word or ending supplies attachment evidence, rather than a complete analysis of the reason clause.
