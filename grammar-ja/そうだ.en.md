# そうだ
## Meaning
**Plain clause + そうだ / そうです** reports information learned from another source. A noun or な-adjective normally keeps its preceding だ.

The active IPADIC parser identifies this そう as a special auxiliary stem. The rule contrasts it with appearance そう, but a morphology-based candidate is not a full quotation or discourse analysis. Standalone そうだ meaning “that is right” is excluded.
