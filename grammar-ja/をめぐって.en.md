# をめぐって

## Meaning

### Matching scope

This rule recognizes the reviewed attachment and marker forms. Its context component is only the observed ending token, or the explicitly reviewed question ending; it does not recover an entire phrase or clause. A basic-form verb plus question particle か is also supported. The reported context is that ending, not a reconstructed full question.

Form evidence is tentative. Literal and grammatical readings can share these words, so the surrounding context determines the intended sense. Only the listed spellings and tokenizations are supported; other endings need separate review. Punctuation and blank paragraphs cannot join the attachment to the marker. Compare the externally resolved explanations below for source-specific meanings.
