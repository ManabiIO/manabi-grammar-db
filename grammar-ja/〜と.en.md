# 〜と

## Meaning

**と** introduces a condition and the event or result that follows. In **春になると花が咲きます**, it describes what normally happens when spring comes. In **家を出ると雨が降った**, it describes what happened when the speaker stepped outside.

A verb or い-adjective can precede **と** directly. **説明が読みやすいと、理解が進みます** means “When the explanation is easy to read, it's easier to understand.” A noun or な-adjective can take **だと** or the polite **ですと**, as in **休日ですと、電車が減ります**.

Polite verbs can take **ますと** or **ませんと**. **書類を提出しますと、画面が変わります** means “When you submit the documents, the screen changes.”

**と** also introduces a quotation, as in **雨が降ると思った** (“I thought it would rain”), and joins nouns, as in **猫と犬** (“cats and dogs”).
