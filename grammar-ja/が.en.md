# が
## Meaning
The particle **が** marks the grammatical subject and often highlights new or contrastive information within a sentence.

The subject can be a quoted expression or clause. Matching checks the case particle's morphology and JMdict identity without requiring an immediately preceding noun. Conjunctive が, used to connect clauses, is excluded. The match selects the particle rather than claiming a full syntactic subject analysis.
### Emphasis
Writers use が to spotlight what precedes it, especially when introducing new topics or answering implicit questions.
