# れる・られる

## Meaning
The suffixes **れる / られる** attach to an irrealis verb form. Depending on the verb and context, they can express passive voice, potential ability, spontaneous experience, or honorific usage. This rule recognizes the shared suffix family; a match does not choose one of those meanings.

The highlighted components separate the local predicate, the suffix and any supported inflected ending. **読ませられた** has **読ませ + られ + た**; the separate causative rule can overlap it. **開催された** has **開催さ + れ + た**, retaining the action noun. Past, polite, negative, progressive and other supported endings remain part of the occurrence, with their own component. A visible suffix can also be recognized in an unfinished fragment without certifying a complete clause.

Coverage requires the certified parser to expose a separate suffix token. Fused potential verbs such as **読める**, contracted causative-passives and other unexposed forms are outside this rule. The attached predicate supports at most one preceding voice suffix. Matches report tentative morphological evidence, not a calibrated probability or a resolved grammatical sense; Japanese context is still needed to interpret every result.
