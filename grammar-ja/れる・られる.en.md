# れる・られる

## Meaning

The suffixes **れる / られる** attach to an irrealis verb form. Depending on the verb and context, they can express passive voice, potential ability, spontaneous experience, or honorific usage.

**開催された** can mean that an event was held. A causative and passive can combine: **読ませられた** can mean “was made to read”. The surrounding sentence determines the intended reading of **れる / られる**.

Potential verbs such as **読める** express ability with a different form. Not every expression of ability contains **れる / られる**.
