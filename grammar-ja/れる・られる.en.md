# れる・られる

## Meaning
The suffixes **れる / られる** attach to an irrealis verb form. Depending on the verb and context, they can express passive voice, potential ability, spontaneous experience, or honorific usage. This rule recognizes the shared suffix family; a match does not choose one of those meanings.

The highlighted components are the immediately attached predicate morphology and the suffix. In **読ませられた**, the immediate attachment is **せ**; the separate causative rule can overlap it. A suru passive selects **さ + れ**, rather than pretending that **さ** is the whole lexical predicate. Trailing past/polite/negative auxiliaries remain outside this family's selection.

Coverage requires the certified parser to expose a separate suffix token. Fused potential verbs such as **読める**, contracted causative-passives and other unexposed forms are outside this rule. Match confidence is unassessed, and Japanese context is needed to interpret every result.
