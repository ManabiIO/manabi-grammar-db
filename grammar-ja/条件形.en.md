# 条件形

## Meaning
**ば** introduces a condition. **安ければ買います** means “I'll buy it if it's cheap.”

With a verb, use the conditional stem, as in **行く → 行けば**. With an い-adjective, replace **い** with **ければ**, as in **安い → 安ければ**. The negative ending **ない** becomes **なければ**, as in **読まない → 読まなければ**.

A noun or な-adjective can use **であれば** or **でなければ**. **予約制でなければ入れます** means “You can go in if reservations aren't required.”
