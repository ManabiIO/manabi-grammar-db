# ために

## Meaning

**ために / 為に** can introduce a purpose, a beneficiary, or a reason. Common contexts include a dictionary-form verb, **noun + の**, and a past verb.

**勉強するために図書館へ行く。** gives a purpose: going to the library to study. **大雨のために電車が遅れた。** gives a reason: the train was delayed because of heavy rain.

Context distinguishes these readings. **に** can be omitted before a pause, as in **調べるため、職員を派遣した。**, a style common in formal and news writing.
