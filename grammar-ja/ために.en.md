# ために
## Meaning
**ために / 為に** can introduce a purpose, a beneficiary, or a reason. Common contexts include a dictionary-form verb, **noun + の**, and a past verb.

The parser cannot reliably decide purpose versus cause from this short pattern alone. Results link to their shared explanation rather than choosing one meaning. The rule also covers a dictionary-form verb followed by **ため、**, a clause connector with に omitted that is common in news prose. Other omitted-particle and inflected forms still need explicit coverage.
