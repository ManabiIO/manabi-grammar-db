# について

## Meaning
**名詞 + について** introduces what a statement, question, or discussion concerns: “about …” or “regarding …”. The noun names the topic.

The current detector requires an attached noun and the relational particle **について**. It also accepts **に就いて** when the parser identifies it as that particle. Employment **仕事に就いている** uses the verb **就く** and is excluded from this rule. Compare **については**, which adds topical **は**.
