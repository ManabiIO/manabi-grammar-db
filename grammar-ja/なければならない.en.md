# なければならない
## Meaning
**なければならない** and **なくてはならない** express a requirement. Use them after a verb to say that an action must be done, as in **返さなければならない** (must return it).

With an い-adjective, use the **く** form. **説明は分かりやすくなければならない** means “The explanation must be easy to understand.”

After **で**, they can describe a required identity or state, as in **原本でなければならない** (it must be the original). With **が** or **は**, they can express that something is required to exist, as in **安全設備がなくてはならない** (there must be safety equipment).

Use **なりません** for the polite ending and **ならなかった** or **なりませんでした** for past requirements. In **雨が降らなければ出かける** (if it does not rain, I will go out), **なければ** introduces an ordinary condition.
