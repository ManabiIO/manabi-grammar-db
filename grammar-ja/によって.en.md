# によって

## Meaning

### Matching scope

This rule requires the reviewed attachment and marker morphology. Context is observed token evidence, not a reconstructed full argument or clause. Depending-on, agency and means readings can share によって. The form match alone does not establish which reading is intended.

Form evidence is tentative. Surrounding context determines the intended sense, and unlisted spellings or endings need separate review. Punctuation and blank paragraphs cannot join attachment and marker. Compare the externally resolved explanations below for the source meanings.
