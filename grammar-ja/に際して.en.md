# に際して

## Meaning

### Matching scope

This rule recognizes the reviewed noun-linked form and exposes the final observed noun token separately from the marker. That token may be only the ending of a compound noun; it is not a recovered whole phrase. Only the fused case-particle tokenization is supported.

Form evidence is tentative. The construction can share wording with literal readings; the surrounding sentence determines the intended meaning. Punctuation and blank paragraphs cannot bridge the noun and marker. Compare the externally resolved explanations below for source-specific senses.
