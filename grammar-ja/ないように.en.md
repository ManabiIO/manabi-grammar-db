# ないように

## Meaning
**動詞のない形 + ように** can state what someone tries to prevent: “so that … does not happen” or “to avoid …”. With **する**, it can describe taking steps to keep that situation from occurring.

### Recognized parts
In **音を立てないように歩く。**, the detector selects **ないように** and returns **立て** separately as the negated predicate. This predicate component is the observed verb stem, not a reconstruction of its dictionary form or the whole clause. The following **歩く** supplies context without becoming part of the highlighted marker.

Both **ないように…** and the comma continuation **ないよう、…** require a following verb. Up to eight intervening noun, particle, adverb, determiner, or prefix tokens allow a short object or modifier. The rule stops at the first verb; it cannot skip an appearance verb to find a later action. Sentence boundaries and quotation boundaries cannot be crossed.

### Contrast
**彼は働かないように見える。** expresses appearance, not prevention. **見える** and **思える** continuations, including their kana spellings and inflected forms, are excluded from this rule. The comma form uses the same check.

This is partial, conservative coverage. Sentence-final requests without a visible continuation, long intervening phrases, and other constructions still need separately reviewed rules. A form match alone does not settle the sentence’s intended meaning; confidence remains unassessed.
