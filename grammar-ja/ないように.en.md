# ないように

## Meaning

**動詞のない形 + ように** can state what someone tries to prevent: “so that … does not happen” or “to avoid …”. With **する**, it can describe taking steps to keep that situation from occurring.

**音を立てないように歩く。** means walking so as not to make a sound. The negative phrase gives the situation to avoid; **歩く** gives the action taken.

### Contrast
**彼は働かないように見える。** expresses appearance: “It looks as though he is not working.” It does not describe an action taken to prevent him from working. The following expression and surrounding context distinguish these readings.
