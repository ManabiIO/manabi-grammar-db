# てもいいですか
## Meaning
**Verb て/で + もいいですか** asks permission to do something. **よい / 良い** are other forms of いい here; **よろしい** is a more formal choice.

The rule covers explicit polite questions with **ですか** or **でしょうか**, including negative actions such as **出さなくてもいいですか**. The applied-action component retains action nouns in **登録してもいいですか** and supported voice morphology. Fused negative **でも** forms require their own grammatical evidence. A verb-tagged いい is not silently reinterpreted as an adjective, so parser-dependent examples can remain unmatched. The rule does not treat every occurrence of ても or いい as a permission question.
