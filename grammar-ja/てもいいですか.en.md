# てもいいですか

## Meaning

**Verb て/で + もいいですか** asks permission to do something. **よい / 良い** are other forms of **いい** here; **よろしい** is a more formal choice.

**登録してもいいですか。** asks whether it is all right to register. **出さなくてもいいですか。** asks whether it is all right not to submit or put something out. **でしょうか** can make the question more tentative.
