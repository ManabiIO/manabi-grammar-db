# 〜しかない
## Meaning

With a verb in its dictionary form, **しかない** means that doing that action is the only available choice: **待つしかない** means “there is nothing to do but wait.”

The linked source also includes **noun + しかない**: only that thing or amount is available. For example, **水しかない** means “there is only water.” This nominal restriction is included on the same grammar page; it does not by itself mean that someone must perform an action.
