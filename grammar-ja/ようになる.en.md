# ようになる

## Meaning

**Verb plain form + ようになる** describes a change in a habit, situation, or ability. Negative verbal context can describe a change toward no longer doing something.

In **夜に出かけないようになった。**, the change is toward no longer going out at night. The final **なる** can itself be inflected or negated; context establishes when the change takes place.

### Contrast
**走るようにした** describes deliberate action or a practice, while **走るように言った** can report an instruction. **そのようになった** instead uses a demonstrative meaning “that way”.
