# ようになる
## Meaning
**Verb plain form + ようになる** describes a change in a habit, situation, or ability. Negative verbal context can describe a change toward no longer doing something.

The final **なる** may itself be inflected or negated. Matching this construction does not prove that the change has already happened.

### Parts and contrasts
The verbal attachment is a separate component; the main selection covers **ように** and the observed form of **なる**. In **夜に出かけないようになった。**, the attachment includes **出かけない**. This expresses a change, so the prevention rule does not claim the same construction.

**走るようにした** describes deliberate action or a practice, while **走るように言った** can report an instruction. Demonstratives such as **そのようになった** and noun comparisons do not satisfy this rule’s verbal attachment. Coverage is conservative and confidence remains unassessed.
