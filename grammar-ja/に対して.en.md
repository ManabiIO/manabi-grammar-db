# に対して

## Meaning

After a noun, **に対して** identifies the person or thing an action, attitude or response is directed toward: “toward,” “to” or “in response to.” **に対し** is a shorter form often used in formal writing. Before another noun, **に対する** describes that noun's relationship to the target.

- 質問に対して答える。 — Answer a question.
- 質問に対する回答 — An answer to a question.

The target can contain several words. In **警察の任意の調べに対し**, the target is the whole phrase “the police's voluntary questioning,” ending immediately before に対し.

### Contrast

**に対して** can also contrast two people, things or situations: “whereas” or “in contrast to.” A preceding clause commonly connects through **のに対して**. The surrounding sentence determines whether the expression indicates a target or a contrast; a highlighted form alone does not establish that distinction.
